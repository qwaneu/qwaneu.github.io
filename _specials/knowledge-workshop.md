Software delivery is knowledge work. Every day, teams learn about technology, architectures, business domains, customer needs and the systems they build. Successful software teams don't just deliver working software that pays the bill - they continuously create, share, and preserve knowledge that will keep them in business in the long term.

Organisations often focus on explicit knowledge only - things that can be written down: code, documentation. But the most valuable knowledge in software delivery lives elsewhere. It's harder to capture, easier to lose, and often determines whether our teams will succeed or struggle in the future: tacit, collective and stigmergic knowledge.

Mapping these different types of knowledge helps reveal where the true value of an organization lies. While explicit knowledge is becoming increasingly accessible through AI, tacit and collective knowledge remain key sources of differentiation, innovation, and long-term effectiveness. By making these forms of knowledge visible, organizations can better protect what makes teams successful and ensure that critical capabilities continue to grow rather than disappear when people, technologies, or priorities change.

## Benefits

For your organisation, this workshop will help to:

- make more effective use of AI, by knowing what knowledge is out of reach for AI;
- identify what knowledge is critical to protect and grow;
- get insight in what knowledge forms a competitive advantage;
- reveal gaps and risks in delivery knowledge management.

## Learning objectives

Through practical exercises and discussion, participants will be introduced to four types of knowledge that are 
essential to successful software delivery: explicit, tacit, collective and stigmergic. 
In groups, participants will explore and discover how this will help your team to effectively grow, transfer and safeguard the knowledge. 
Participants will see new opportunities, recognise hidden risks, and identify interventions that strengthen 
(or undermine!) team effectiveness.
