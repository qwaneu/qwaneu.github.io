From README.md "Styling": "ribbon op mobile (smal) aanpassen; nu valt het blog titel deel buiten beeld" (on narrow mobile the blog title part falls outside the viewport).

Where: _includes/header_clean.html:26-33 (.ribbon with .ribbon__element children) and _sass/_breadcrumbs.scss.

What happens: the breadcrumb is a horizontal row of anchors; long post titles do not wrap or truncate, so the row pushes the document wider than the viewport. Every blog post has this.

Interaction with other yaks: d1-ribbon-anchor replaces the last, href-less anchor with a span — probably necessary for a clean fix, so do this after or together with it, not before. d3-viewport-scale's 0.86 may be masking exactly this. If d3 lands first, this defect becomes more visible, which is the point.

Done when: a post with a long title has no horizontal overflow at 320/390, the full title can still be read (wrapping or a truncation that exposes the full text to assistive tech), and the ribbon keeps its visual style at 768+.
