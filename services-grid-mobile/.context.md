From README.md "Styling": "mobile weergave diensten pagina: grid met diensten valt buiten beeld" (services page on mobile: the services grid falls outside the viewport).

Where: _sass/_services.scss and _sass/_qwan-overrides.scss (.courses-grid uses `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(23em, 0.33fr))` — 23em minimum is ~368px at 16px, so at 390px width with any padding the track cannot shrink and the grid overflows). Also the .partners-list grid (`grid-template-columns: 2fr 6fr`, `column-gap: 5em`).

Why it matters: horizontal overflow on the page that sells the services. It also explains the viewport hack in d3-viewport-scale.

Check all grids, not just services: courses-grid, partnerners-list, contactus-grid, blogpage-layout. Use minmax(min(23em, 100%), ...) or a media query that collapses to one column; do not fix it by scaling the viewport.

Done when: no horizontal scrollbar at 320/390/768 for /consulting, /training, /partners, /contact, /blog and the service pages, verified from rendered pages, not just the CSS.
