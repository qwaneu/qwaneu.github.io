If you have other questions or challenges about your delivery process or your teams, 
or if you are not yet sure what question to ask, 
we can also deliver consulting services tailored to your specific needs. 

We will from where you are, not from methods and frameworks. 
We bring fresh perspectives on what is happening. 
We will use existing methods and frameworks when and where appropriate.

## Benefits

- better understanding of next steps that bring actual value;
- increased situational awareness;
- focus on your organisation's needs;
- advice from a practitioner's perspective, grounded in experience.

## Approach

We start with an in-depth conversation about what is happening in your situation 
and what you are observing. We will also discuss what this means for you and what is valuable to you and your 
organization. 

Based on this conversation, we can propose one or more next steps, including: 

- run an in-depth systemic thinking workshop to find the systemic patterns that make change hard;
- visit teams and observe what is actually happening, from a fresh perspective;
- run a sensemaking workshop to gain insights into what decision-making processes;
- do a knowledge mapping session to uncover what skills and expertise your organization has and what you can do to 
  keep and grow these;
- introducing you to multiple, different perspectives on delivery, helping you to observe what was previously not 
  visible.
