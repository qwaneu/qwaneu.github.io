> Proficient teams enable software delivery excellence.

We support teams getting started with good engineering practices or taking their next step.  
After attending a training course, developers are enthusiastic. Usually getting started does not take that much effort. 
Embedding new practices like test-driven development, agile working, merciless refactoring, working effectively with legacy code 
in the day-to-day work, and practicing until a team becomes proficient can be a challenge, especially when there is pressure to deliver.

We guide teams to become proficient through team practice sessions and mentoring through pair programming.
Teams will learn how to apply new practices in their situation and their own code.

Team coaching and mentoring help make better development practices stick.

A team coaching trajectory usually takes 3 to 6 months with our coaches working off and on with the team. The total 
amount of time and effort depends on factors like team size, skill level, and code base complexity. 
A team working on a large legacy code base will also need to learn specific practices for dealing with legacy code.

Our technical coaches focus on improving team skills. We aim to make ourselves redundant: after a team coaching trajectory, 
the team should be able to apply the new practices on their own.

We recommend starting with 2 or more days of training to get everyone on the same page.

If you are unsure what support your teams need and what you can do to help them become more effective, we recommend
doing a [team proficiency scan](/consulting/team-scan). This will provide a clear direction of what is most valuable 
to do first.
