## Approach

In general, an architecture review consists of these steps:

* Kick-off session to determine scope, objectives, and stakeholders
* Interviews with stakeholders
* Inspection of code, documents, CI/CD pipelines, and other materials
* Intermediate reporting of findings
* Presentation of findings to stakeholders
* Delivery of an architecture review report

Alternatively, we can do a short project audit for you, where we
investigate a project based on your questions, through interviews,
observation, and inspection of code, documents, and other materials.
We report our findings back to you. Based on our findings, we propose how we can proceed, for example, with hands on
mentoring, on-on-one coaching, or additional training.
